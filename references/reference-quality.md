# Reference quality and observed rejection

## Intended reference look
Use a light standard 2D Apple map, one rounded landscape ground surface, a compact white Memoji balloon with blue dot, and a few white controls. Dark/no-avatar variants are optional user choices; they do not match this reference by default. A request to resume the reference after a rejected variant supersedes the earlier variant choice.

## Map proportions and readability
- Obtain enough map area at an appropriate zoom; favor visible local street/building structure over a large district overview. Verify the named landmark before capture.
- Crop to a landscape aspect without changing geography. Resize uniformly, and transform the map, bubble and controls together. Do not stretch a portrait viewport or narrow map strip to fill a wide panel.
- Fit the plane to the source stance, then inspect whether the far/near edge compression still permits readable labels and a recognizable face. Rotation does not require extreme vertical compression. Reduce panel width, increase its visible depth, or change framing when needed; keep the original feet fixed.
- Inspect the actual final image, not only a corner matrix. Circular controls and the face should not become thin ovals. Keep the primary landmark label readable at delivery size and place the bubble away from foreground occlusion.
- Keep attribution visible. A MapKit snapshot supplies map imagery, not every native Maps app control; reference-inspired controls are separately authored overlays. Never add a location dot as evidence of the user's real GPS location.

## Demonstrated failure to avoid
In a Chengdu IFS preview, a cropped Apple web screenshot was uniformly prepared but then heavily foreshortened into a long shallow quadrilateral. The user rejected the stretched appearance, reduced readability, dark palette and absence of avatar. That output is not an approved anchor. Its exact photo coordinates and city are not reusable defaults.

## Portability and delivery
Package the actual visual anchor, provenance for reusable avatar assets, applicable acquisition methods and quality checks. Do not rely on chat history or temporary clipboard paths. A successful asset acquisition or package validator is not proof of human aesthetic approval. Finish one composed preview after acquisition; distinguish unavailable assets, preview status and verified source fidelity.
